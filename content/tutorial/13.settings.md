View your profile, choose an interface language, and customize how relationship cardinality is
displayed.

## Open Settings

Select your avatar in the application header and choose **Settings**. The page has **User profile**
and **Diagram settings** sections. Each section has its own Save action: change the active section's
controls, then save before switching sections.

Save is enabled when that section differs from its saved values. It is disabled while saving.
Switching sections reloads the selected section from saved preferences, so unsaved edits in the
section you leave are not retained.

::

## Profile information and language

User profile displays your avatar, name, and email from your account. These values are informational
on this page; it does not currently offer editing the name, uploading a new avatar, changing email
or password, password reset, or deleting the account.

Choose **English**, **Ukrainian**, or **Polish** from Language and select Save. The interface
updates to the saved language, and localized labels and relative dates follow it. This does not
translate schema names, note contents, AI messages already in the conversation, or your teammates'
preferences.

The tutorial is Ukrainian when your interface language is Ukrainian, and English for every other
language, including while you are signed out.

## Diagram settings

The **Show relation cardinality** switch controls whether relationship notation and endpoint markers
appear on the canvas. Turn it off for a simpler view, or on to inspect the intended relationship
counts. Select Save to apply the preference.

This is a user display setting, not a change to the schema. Relations retain their cardinality data
and still export foreign keys. Other users can have different display preferences. To edit a
relationship's cardinality while the visual labels are hidden, open it through the Relations panel.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** from the avatar menu to end your session. The app reloads afterward. Save
personal diagrams and check that team diagrams show Saved first. Signing out does not delete your
account, projects, membership, or saved versions. You can still read the tutorial and open valid
public share links while signed out.
