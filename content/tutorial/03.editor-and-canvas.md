Find your way around the editor, navigate large diagrams, arrange objects, and use the canvas
controls.

## The editor at a glance

::tutorial-media{src='/tutorial/media/editor-overview.png' alt='Editor showing the header, left sidebar, canvas controls, and minimap'}
::

The **header** contains the home logo, SQL export, public sharing when available, the project name,
tutorial shortcut, collaboration indicators, version history, Save, and your profile menu. The
tutorial book opens the guide in a new tab so you can keep the diagram open.

The narrow **sidebar toolbar** switches between Tables, Relations, and AI. AI is available to users
with edit permission. The larger sidebar panel shows the selected tool. Use the panel button just
outside its top-right edge to collapse or reopen the sidebar and gain canvas space.

The **canvas** shows tables, lines between fields, groups, and sticky notes. The controls are at the
bottom left; the minimap is at the bottom right. Some header tools are hidden on narrow screens: use
a wider window for SQL export, sharing, and renaming the project.

## Pan, zoom, and fit

Drag an empty part of the canvas to pan. Use the plus and minus controls to zoom in or out;
scrolling over the canvas also changes the zoom. Zooming changes your view without changing field
types or other schema data.

**Fit view** centers the diagram and chooses a zoom that shows its objects. The initial view also
fits a nonempty diagram when it opens. The minimap gives you an overview of a larger schema; drag or
zoom within it to navigate.

To find one specific table, hover over its row in the Tables panel and use **Fit to screen**. If a
search filter hides it, clear the filter first.

## Move and select objects

Drag a table by its header to change its position. Click the small expand icon at the right of its
header to open the table in the sidebar, where its fields can be edited. Clicking the table itself
toggles selection and reveals connection handles while editing is enabled. Drag a note using its top
strip or a group using the non-input area of its header. Text inputs are for editing, not dragging.

Select a relationship line to highlight it. Selected relations can be removed with Delete. Notes and
groups can also be selected and removed with Delete. Tables use their sidebar trash control; Delete
is not a general table deletion shortcut.

The lock/unlock control toggles canvas interaction, which is useful when navigating without moving
objects. It is not a permission setting or a project lock for other members. Read-only viewers
cannot enable editing with this control.

## Put objects in front or behind

Right-click a table, note, or group and choose **Bring to front** or **Send to back**. This changes
its stacking order when objects overlap. Use this to keep a group behind its tables or a note
visible above a crowded area.

Click outside the menu, move the canvas, or press Escape to dismiss the context menu. Stacking order
and object positions are part of the diagram and follow the normal save behavior.

## Read-only mode

A team viewer can inspect the diagram and its tables and relationships, but editing controls are
hidden or disabled. The public share page shows the canvas without the full editing sidebar or
header. Pan, zoom, and fit help viewers explore either kind of diagram. If you expect to edit a team
diagram, ask an admin to check your role and wait for collaboration to finish connecting.

See [Keyboard shortcuts and troubleshooting](/tutorial/reference) for the exact keys supported by
the editor.
